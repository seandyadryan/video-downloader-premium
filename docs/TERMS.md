# Responsible use

Download only material you own, material licensed for downloading, or material for which the rights holder has given permission. Respect applicable copyright law and the terms of the websites you visit.

The app supports standard HTTPS video files and accessible HTML5 video sources. It does not promise to download from every website, bypass DRM/paywalls, download YouTube videos, decrypt protected streams, or supply video content. Sites may change their behavior or deny downloads.

Private DNS is an Android system setting. It encrypts DNS queries and may filter some domains; it does not provide a VPN tunnel, change your country, or hide your IP address. Availability depends on the device, network and AdGuard service.

The application is intended to be sold as a one-time paid app through Google Play. The publisher must configure pricing and countries before launch. Purchases/refunds are governed by the publisher terms and applicable Google Play policies. Fill in publisher legal details and obtain an appropriate regional review before commercial distribution.
