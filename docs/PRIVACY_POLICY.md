# Video Downloader Premium — Privacy Policy

Video Downloader Premium does not operate a developer backend, collect analytics, show advertisements, or require an app account. The developer does not receive your browsing history, download history, bookmarks, or saved videos through the app.

## Data on your device

The application stores settings, bookmarks, browser tabs, browsing history, and download history in its private Android storage. Browser history contains up to 500 recent website URLs, titles, and visit times. Up to eight open tabs store their last URL, title, and home-page state so you can reopen them. URLs can contain temporary access tokens. Download history includes file names, Android download identifiers, and source URLs. Android cloud backup for the application is disabled.

The embedded Android WebView may store cookies, cached pages, navigation history and website storage locally. Clear history in the browser removes the local browsing history and each live tab's back/forward list. Clear browsing data in Settings removes history, cookies, cache, and website storage, and resets open tabs to Home. Bookmarks and download history are separate and can be removed individually. Uninstalling clears private application data; downloaded files in public Downloads remain until you delete them.

Downloaded videos are saved by Android DownloadManager under Downloads / Video Downloader Premium. The app can view progress, remove its downloads, and share/open completed videos only when you ask. Removal deletes the associated file.

## Network requests and third parties

When you search, browse or download, your device connects directly to the selected search engine, visited websites and download servers. These services receive ordinary network data such as your IP address, URL, user agent, and applicable cookies. Their own privacy policies apply. The application does not proxy traffic through developer servers.

Cookies are excluded from third-party WebView contexts. A download to the same host as the current HTTPS page may receive applicable cookies and a Referer header. Cross-host downloads do not receive the page cookies through the application. The operating system manages subsequent download requests and redirects.

If you enable Private DNS in Android settings, Android sends DNS queries to `dns.adguard-dns.com`, operated by AdGuard. Consult [AdGuard DNS privacy policy](https://adguard-dns.io/en/privacy.html). Private DNS does not conceal your public IP or create a VPN. The application only opens the system settings and observes the active connection status.

Sharing a completed video sends it to the app you choose. Your chosen recipient app controls subsequent processing. Google Play processes purchases under its own policies; this application does not collect payment information.

## Permissions

Internet access is needed for browsing and downloading. Network state is used to show Private DNS status. Public download files are managed through Android DownloadManager; the application does not request broad file access, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or VPN-service permissions.

## Choices

You can choose the search provider, app language, Wi-Fi-only downloading, and website JavaScript. You can clear browser data, delete bookmarks, remove download entries and files, or uninstall the app. You can change system Private DNS at any time through Android settings.

For privacy questions, contact the publisher using the support contact above. Update this policy if any data-processing feature or SDK is introduced.
