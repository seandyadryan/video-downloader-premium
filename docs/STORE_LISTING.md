# Listing drafts

## English

Name: **Video Downloader Premium**

Short description: **Browser tabs and history. Save permitted videos. One purchase, no in-app ads.**

Final English copy for Play Console: [short description](store-assets/short-description-en.txt) and [full description](store-assets/full-description-en.txt). See [upload assets](store-assets/ASSETS.md) for the six phone and six desktop screenshots and feature graphic.

Browse the web and build your own video collection with a native Android experience. Search with DuckDuckGo, Google or Bing, open your favorite websites, keep up to eight tabs open, and revisit device-local history. Discover accessible HTML5 video files. Paste a direct HTTPS video link or share one from another app to start a download.

Follow progress in Downloads, let Android handle background transfers, and open or share completed videos with compatible apps. Use Wi-Fi-only downloading, local bookmarks, an illustrated guide, and 110 offline language choices.

The Private DNS menu guides you to Android settings for `dns.adguard-dns.com` and reports the actual active status. Private DNS encrypts DNS requests; it does not hide your IP or create a VPN tunnel.

One Play Store purchase. No developer ads, subscriptions, analytics, or app account. Only save videos you own or have permission to download. DRM, protected/live streams, YouTube downloads and some embedded sources are unsupported. Playback requires a compatible video-player app. An Internet connection is required for browsing and downloading.

## Bahasa Indonesia

Nama: **Video Downloader Premium**

Deskripsi singkat: **Telusuri dan simpan video yang diizinkan. Sekali beli, tanpa iklan.**

Jelajahi web dan bangun koleksi video Anda dengan aplikasi Android native. Pilih mesin pencari DuckDuckGo, Google, atau Bing. Temukan sumber video HTML5 yang dapat diakses, tempel tautan video HTTPS langsung, atau bagikan tautan dari aplikasi lain.

Pantau progres unduhan, gunakan opsi Wi-Fi saja, simpan bookmark, dan ikuti panduan bergambar. Tersedia 110 pilihan bahasa/lokal secara offline. Buka atau bagikan video yang selesai diunduh melalui aplikasi yang kompatibel.

Menu Private DNS membantu pengaturan `dns.adguard-dns.com` dan menampilkan status koneksi sebenarnya. Private DNS mengenkripsi permintaan DNS, bukan menyembunyikan IP atau membuat koneksi VPN.

Sekali beli melalui Play Store, tanpa iklan dari pengembang atau langganan. Unduh hanya video milik Anda atau video yang diizinkan untuk disimpan. DRM, streaming terlindungi/langsung, unduhan YouTube, dan sebagian sumber video tertanam tidak didukung.
